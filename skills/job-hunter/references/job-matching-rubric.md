# Job Matching Rubric

Use this rubric for target-role hypotheses and job shortlist triage. Scores support discussion; they do not replace user judgment.

## Role Cluster Output

For each role cluster, include:

- Role names.
- Why it may fit.
- Evidence from experience assets.
- Major gaps.
- 30-day proof-building actions.
- Suitable company types.
- Jobs to avoid.

## Score Breakdown

Total 100:

- 40: real experience to job-requirement fit.
- 20: hard requirements satisfied.
- 15: user interest and long-term direction.
- 15: practical constraints: city, availability, degree, internship duration, salary.
- 10: risk screen. Award points when risk is low; subtract for fuzzy role, suspicious company, training-loan signals, impossible requirements, or poor fit.

## Priority Bands

| Score | Band | Action |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 80-100 | A | High priority. Tailor resume and prepare application. |
| 70-79 | B | Apply if user interest is high and gaps are manageable. |
| 60-69 | C | Observation pool. Improve proof or choose selectively. |
| <60 | D | Do not recommend applying now. Explain why. |

## Required Fields For Job Matching

- Job title and company.
- Platform/source and observation date.
- JD text or extracted requirements.
- User's matching evidence.
- Must-have gaps.
- Resume focus.
- Next action.

## Red Flags

- "无经验高薪", "交钱培训", "先贷款", "包就业".
- Job title is attractive but work content is sales, telemarketing, or unrelated.
- Requirements conflict with user's hard constraints.
- Company or JD is too vague to tailor a resume.
