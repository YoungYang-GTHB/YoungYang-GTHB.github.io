# Interview Training

V1 supports text or agent voice-input transcripts. Do not require audio processing.

## Self-Introduction Review

Assess:

- Structure: who I am, target role, relevant evidence, why this company/role.
- Specificity: concrete actions and results instead of adjectives.
- Job fit: connects experience to JD keywords.
- Length: 60-120 seconds for most interviews.
- Risk: fabricated claims, unsupported numbers, overpromising, negative framing.
- Clarity: short sentences, few filler phrases, clear transitions.

## Simulation Protocol

1. Ask one question only.
2. Wait for the user's answer.
3. Ask up to two follow-up questions based on weak evidence or vague wording.
4. Review the answer with:
   - what worked;
   - what was unclear;
   - what not to say;
   - stronger STAR version;
   - one practice drill.

## Common Question Sets

- 自我介绍。
- 为什么投这个岗位？
- 讲一个项目经历。
- 你在团队中具体做了什么？
- 你遇到过什么困难，怎么解决？
- 你的优势和短板是什么？
- 为什么选择这个城市/行业/公司？
- 你还有什么问题想问我们？

## STAR Story Record

```text
### Story: <故事名>
- Question types:
- Situation:
- Task:
- Action:
- Result:
- Evidence:
- Job keywords:
- Follow-up details:
- Risky wording to avoid:
- Improved answer:
```

## Refusal Boundary

If the user asks for fake stories, say no. Offer to mine real school, project, self-study, competition, volunteer, or part-time experiences instead.
