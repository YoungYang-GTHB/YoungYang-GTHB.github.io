# Visual system

Use 16:9 widescreen with a restrained technical aesthetic.

- Canvas: warm off-white `F6F8FB`; dark sections `0B1220`.
- Primary ink: `10233E`; secondary ink: `526176`.
- Accent cyan: `19B5C5`; evidence blue: `3A72F8`; caution amber: `F4A340`.
- Chinese font: Noto Sans CJK SC. English font: Liberation Sans.
- Default body size: 18–22 pt; footnotes: at least 10 pt.

Make each slide visually distinct but structurally consistent. Prefer a system diagram,
metric comparison, experimental funnel, timeline, or one strong image over repeated
card grids. Charts must remain editable PowerPoint objects or native shapes.

Use conclusion-style titles, visible units, direct labels, and short source/measurement
notes. Avoid decorative icons, gradients that reduce contrast, and unexplained acronyms.

After rendering, inspect both a contact sheet and individual PNGs. Check projector
legibility at 50% zoom, CJK font substitution, cropped images, chart labels, page number,
and whether the visual hierarchy still works in grayscale.
